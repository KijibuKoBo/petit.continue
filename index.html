Our Story

From Full-Scale Elegance
to Miniature Masterpieces

For over five decades, Matsunaga Kobo has crafted antique-style furniture in Shizuoka — Japan's historic center of fine woodworking. Our full-size brand, CONTINUE, is beloved for its flowing curves, hand-carved details, and signature cabriole legs.

In 2023, we channeled that same passion into Petit.Continue — faithfully recreating every sculpted curve, every hand-carved flourish, at 1/3 scale. These are not toys. They are miniature works of furniture art, made with the same techniques, the same woods, and the same hands that build our full-size pieces.

Each piece fits 40–60 cm dolls, from SD to DD and beyond — transforming your doll's world into an atmosphere of timeless elegance.
